# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We want this project to be useful to people with different levels of GitHub Actions, security, design, and documentation experience. Participants are expected to be respectful, specific, and willing to revise an idea when evidence changes.

Welcome behavior includes asking clarifying questions, explaining tradeoffs, crediting prior work, and separating criticism of a proposal from criticism of a person. Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, sexualized conduct, deliberate disruption, and publishing private information without permission.

## In technical discussions

Assume good intent while asking for evidence. Prefer a reproducible example over a sweeping claim. When discussing quota or billing, distinguish current documentation from remembered behavior and label uncertainty. Do not pressure maintainers to merge an untested workflow or contributors to disclose private organizational information in public.

When reviewing design, criticize the behavior and constraints rather than a person's taste. When reviewing payment or security changes, be direct about risk without turning the conversation into personal accusation.

## Maintainer responsibilities

Maintainers should explain decisions, keep review standards consistent, protect reporters' privacy, and avoid using project authority to extract unrelated labor or personal information. A maintainer may pause a discussion when it becomes hostile or when the same concern has already been answered.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, remove, or decline contributions that violate this standard. Repeated or severe violations may result in temporary or permanent exclusion from project spaces. Decisions should be proportionate, documented privately, and focused on restoring a healthy project environment.

Report conduct concerns privately to **conduct@fedpromptly.com**. Security issues belong under [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Include links, dates, and context when safe to do so.
