# Authors and Contributors

Actions Budget Policy is a community-maintained project in the FED-OS / FedPromptly ecosystem. The repository contains policy prose, workflow examples, shell tooling, and static-site content; contributions may therefore be credited for engineering, writing, design, accessibility, research, or operational review.

## Project authorship

The initial project direction and repository stewardship belong to **FED-OS / FedPromptly**. Individual contributors retain credit for their original contributions while accepting the repository's license and contribution standards.

## How credit is recorded

A contributor may be listed here when they make a meaningful, accepted contribution and choose to be identified. GitHub history remains the detailed record of authorship. Maintainers should not add a person's name without their consent.

## Contributor expectations

Contributions should be original or properly attributed. Do not submit copied provider documentation, licensed images without permission, private organization material, or generated content that makes unsupported claims about pricing or legal requirements. See [CITATIONS.md](CITATIONS.md) when a change relies on external documentation.

If your contribution is missing from this page, open a small documentation pull request or contact the maintainers. The goal is accurate credit, not a leaderboard.
