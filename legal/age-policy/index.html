Age Confirmation Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-26

What this gate is

It is a self-declared confirmation that you are 18 or older before payment links appear. The choice is stored in your browser's localStorage.

What it is not

It is not KYC, a government ID check, or a legal age-verification service. The confirmation is not transmitted to us.

Why we use it

It keeps payment pages away from minors and makes the site's expectations visible before an external processor loads.

Clearing the gate

Clear this site's localStorage or browser site data if you confirmed by mistake. You can continue reading the documentation without confirming.
