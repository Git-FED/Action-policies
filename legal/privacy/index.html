Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-26

Summary

This static site does not run analytics, set its own cookies, or create user accounts. External donation processors may collect information under their own policies. We receive only information you choose to send us or that a processor makes available, such as a name and donation amount.

What we collect

We do not run analytics and do not set cookies for tracking. The age confirmation uses browser localStorage only; it is not sent to us. If you email us, we receive the contents of that message and the address needed to reply.

Donations

PayPal, Stripe, Ko-fi, Buy Me a Coffee, and NOWPayments operate their own payment experiences and privacy policies. We do not receive your card number.

Hosting

The site may be hosted through GitHub Pages and Cloudflare Pages. Those providers may process technical logs such as IP addresses under their own policies.

Your rights

You may request access, correction, or deletion of information you sent directly to us by emailing support@fedpromptly.com.

Children

The site is not directed at children under 13. Payment pages include an 18+ self-declared age confirmation.

Changes

We update the date above when this policy changes.
