Policy guide

Quota policy

Short version: Actions minutes and storage are shared resources. Narrow triggers, cancel obsolete runs, retain artifacts briefly, and verify current billing rules before making promises.

Why this exists

A quota incident interrupts engineering feedback. The practical response is not to panic-disable every check; it is to protect the checks that answer the most important questions and remove work that no longer answers any question.

The six defaults

  1. Use the narrowest trigger and path filter that remains trustworthy.
  2. Cancel superseded pull-request runs.
  3. Skip expensive work on draft pull requests.
  4. Set artifact retention explicitly to three days or less.
  5. Bound every job with a timeout and avoid unbounded polling.
  6. Require review before adding a platform matrix or self-hosted runner.

Verify it

Open Organization Settings → Billing and plans → Actions, then run the local scanners and review the largest consumers weekly. Exact plan limits and billing behavior change; the official dashboard remains authoritative.
